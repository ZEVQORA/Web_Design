# ZEVQORA brand rules

The canonical sources are `references/brand-board.png` and
`references/zev-turnaround.png`. When this document and a reference disagree,
the reference wins.

## Locked — never redesign

- the Z mark
- the ZEVQORA wordmark
- Zev, his proportions and his personality
- the logo lockup geometry
- the core palette

## Palette

Only these four are brand colours. Do not invent additional ones.

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#0F1115` | type, inverted surfaces |
| Cloud | `#F7F8FA` | canvas |
| Soft Blue | `#468BFF` | accent — scarce, and reserved |
| Stone | `#E6E9EE` | hairlines and separation |

Tonal steps are derived from these four. Semantic state colours (verified,
rejected, warning) are deliberately desaturated so they read as instrument
readings rather than alerts.

**Soft Blue is a signal, not a decoration.** It carries the single primary
action and verification state. If everything on a screen is blue, nothing is.

## Voice

ZEVQORA is precise, calm, expensive, editorial, technical and confident.

Never claim customers, production savings or revenue that do not exist. Never
resurrect the unsupported ~45% claim. Where a number appears, its qualifier
appears with it — including that the internal benchmark's confidence interval
crosses zero.

## Zev

Zev is scarce on purpose. Strong presence in the hero; small and iconic in the
loader; light in auth; a brief helper moment on download; an assistant presence
in the account area; essentially absent from pricing.

Scarcity is what keeps him feeling intentional rather than mascot-driven.
